# Channel copy

Enabled channel in this version: **ReadMe changelog**, see [changelog](references/changelog.md). Demo clip captions (see `demo-recording`) follow the rules for every channel below. Do not draft email, LinkedIn, blog or sales copy yet. If asked, say those channels are not part of this version.

## Rules for every channel

1. **Facts come from the ticket.** Use only what the story, its acceptance criteria and its comments say. Do not invent features, numbers, speed or accuracy claims, plans, prices, dates, availability or customer names. If a fact you need is missing, leave it out and list it as an open question in your result. Never write a placeholder like TBD or TODO into a draft.
2. **Write for the customer.** Say what they can now do and why it helps. Plain words, short sentences, active voice. No ticket keys, no internal team or system names, no feature flag names, no words like epic, sprint or backlog.
3. **No promises about the future.** Shipped work only: describe what exists today. No "soon", "will", "coming", or roadmap hints.
4. **One idea per entry.** A headline entry covers one story. The grouped entry lists each supporting story as one short bullet.
5. **Say where to find it** only when the ticket says where. If it does not, do not guess.
6. **Plain style.** No emoji, no marketing superlatives, no filler like "we're excited to announce".
7. **Human review is not optional.** Your draft is a proposal. Say in your result what you are least sure of.

## Review checks

The reviewer checks every draft against the ticket line by line. A draft fails review when it:

- contains a claim the ticket does not support,
- contains a ticket key, internal name, placeholder or future-tense promise,
- misstates what changed or who it is for,
- says more than the story delivers,
- or does not match the title, type and slug in its header.
